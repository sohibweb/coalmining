## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system defines an authoritative, institutional, and forward-looking presence for a tier-one natural resource and energy enterprise. It balances heavy-industry operational strength with the transparency, technological rigor, and stewardship required of modern publicly traded energy leaders. The aesthetic rejects raw brutalism in favor of an architectural, grounded luxury: clean planar compositions, structural precision, and rich terrestrial tones.

### Target Audience
- Institutional investors, energy fund analysts, and regulatory bodies requiring operational transparency, financial disclosures, and governance metrics.
- Global trading partners, logistics operators, and downstream commodity buyers seeking enterprise capacity data.
- Prospective technical talent, local community stakeholders, and ESG auditors reviewing sustainability and environmental accountability initiatives.

### Emotional Response
The interface evokes stability, grounded authority, and uncompromising execution. The warm earth tones replace sterile corporate blues with mineral warmth and natural substance, communicating scale, durability, and commercial confidence.

### Design Movement
**Architectural Modern Industrial:** High-precision layout grids, structured hairline framing, strict rectangular forms, crisp informational densities, and tactile organic warmth derived from earth-derived chromatic foundations.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette is rooted in subterranean and energy-producing mineral elements: raw terracotta clay, calcined amber, dark anthracite charcoal, and warm calcified ivory.

- **Primary Accent (`#D4621A` - Terracotta):** Represents thermal energy, industrial machinery, and oxidized earth. Deployed strategically for key conversion targets, active states, progress indicators, and critical metrics.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F4AE52` - Warm Amber/Gold):** Evokes refined energy, sunlight, and metallurgical warmth. Used for secondary tier badges, sub-charts, highlight borders, and hovered interactive states.
- **Tertiary & Dominant Dark Surface (`#2A1A0E` - Dark Espresso / Charcoal Mineral):** The primary text color in light mode and the background for high-impact industrial segments (such as header hero zones, metric counters, footers, and structural navigation).
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FEF3E2` - Warm Light Ivory):** The foundational substrate across default pages, replacing stark sterile white with an organic, sunlit parchment tone that softens reading contrast while preserving high readability.

### Semantic & Surface Rules
- **Surface Canvas (Default):** `#FEF3E2`
- **Surface Elevated (Card / Panel):** `#FFFFFF` (resting on `#FEF3E2` with hairline separation)
- **Surface Dark (Enterprise Section):** `#2A1A0E` with text rendered in `#FEF3E2` and accents in `#F4AE52`
- **Border Subtle (Light):** `#EADCC8` (1px clean hairline)
- **Border Structural (Light):** `#D9C3A5`
- **Border Subtle (Dark Surface):** `rgba(254, 243, 226, 0.15)`
- **Success:** `#2D6A4F` (Forest Jade)
- **Alert / Notice:** `#D97706` (Mineral Amber)
- **Destructive:** `#B91C1C` (Deep Crimson)

## Typography

### Hierarchy & Typographic Voice
The system leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent titles, banners, figures, and structural metadata. Its clean geometry provides an assertive, institutional poise. For informational reading, tables, analytical disclosures, and executive body copy, **Inter** provides neutral, dense, and hyper-legible text performance across rigorous data grids.

### Localization & Indonesian Copy Styling
All interface copy is composed in formal, standard Bahasa Indonesia (Ragam Formal Baku). 
- Headlines use direct, definitive corporate phrases (misalnya: *Operasi Pertambangan Berkelanjutan*, *Kinerja Keuangan & Tata Kelola Perusahaan*).
- Navigation and metric tags apply `label-caps` in uppercase styling with deliberate tracking (misalnya: *LAPORAN TAHUNAN*, *PENGEMBANGAN MASYARAKAT*, *PRODUKSI BATUBARA*).
- Operational figures and metrics are styled using tabular figures (tnum) to ensure numerical comparisons remain vertically aligned in tables and dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

### Architectural Grid
The layout system enforces a structured, architectural 12-column grid system optimized for desktop-first enterprise portals with maximum viewport containment at `1440px`.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, 80px side margins. Outer container max-width: 1360px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 40px side margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px side margins.

### Spacing Cadence & Rhythm
Vertical spacing utilizes an intentional multi-tier rhythm to clearly separate structural narrative shifts from functional element clusters:
- **Major Section Cadence (`space-3xl` / 96px):** Separates distinct thematic blocks (misalnya: Ringkasan ESG to Operasi Tambang).
- **Module Cadence (`space-2xl` / 64px):** Separates header groupings from underlying multi-card layouts.
- **Card Internal Padding (`space-lg` / 24px or `space-xl` / 40px):** Provides breathing room within bordered modules.

## Elevation & Depth

### Structural Boundary Philosophy
This design system avoids soft, floating dropshadows. Depth is established through **architectural stratification, crisp surface tone shifts, and structural hairline borders**. This communicates stability, permanence, and operational groundedness.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Base Substrate):** The foundational `#FEF3E2` canvas. Non-elevated, grounding the entire screen.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#FEF3E2`. Defined by a 1px solid border in `#EADCC8`. No shadow applied.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyout):** On hover or dropdown reveal, the panel retains its solid border and gains a precise, low-deflection shadow tinted with mineral charcoal: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(42, 26, 14, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(42, 26, 14, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Critical Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by `#2A1A0E` 1px border with an authoritative architectural drop: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(42, 26, 14, 0.22)`. Accompanied by a backdrop overlay in `rgba(42, 26, 14, 0.65)`.
5. **Inverted Industrial Tier (Dark Container):** Monolithic `#2A1A0E` background with interior subdivisions bounded by `rgba(254, 243, 226, 0.12)` borders.

## Shapes

### Strict Precision Form Language
The roundedness level is strictly **0 (Sharp)** across all interface tokens.

- **Corner Radius:** `0px` on all buttons, cards, tags, input elements, modals, table rows, and image containers.
- **Rationale:** Industrial engineering and mining logistics require structural authority. Right-angled geometry creates architectural precision, aligns seamlessly to rigid grids, and distinguishes the enterprise from generic, bubbly consumer tech templates.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Always crisp, solid 1px lines. Accent borders (e.g., active tabs, lead metrics) employ an intentional 3px or 4px linear marker to emphasize priority without altering corner geometry.
